# Fictional monthly reducing-balance product

All money is signed `BIGINT` satang; external financial amounts are decimal-digit JSON strings, and Vue uses `BigInt` for exact formatting. Incoming payment amounts must be positive and at most 100,000,000,000,000 satang. Rate calculations use C# decimal; period interest rounds to the nearest satang with ties away from zero. Checked arithmetic prevents overflow from posting corrupted balances.

Origination freezes the complete contractual schedule. Monthly interest is scheduled opening principal × annual rate / 12. Principal is the lesser of scheduled opening principal and payment less interest. Dates are the last day of each successive month, including February/leap years. The final installment clears the scheduled principal. Unsupported negative rates, nonamortizing payments, and schedules beyond 100 years are rejected.

LOAN-1001: 10,000,000 satang opening principal, 0.12 annual rate, 900,000 satang monthly payment, first due 2026-10-31. First scheduled interest is 100,000, principal 800,000, contractual closing principal 9,200,000. Actual principal changes only when allocated principal posts.

Only installments whose due date is at or before the bank paidAt business date are eligible. Business dates use Asia/Bangkok; all stored timestamps use UTC. The displayed demo cutoff is 2026-10-31 independently of the computer's actual date. Allocate oldest eligible installment first, interest before principal. Partial payments retain unpaid amounts. Any remainder becomes an unapplied customer credit liability requiring review; it does not silently pay future principal. Contractual interest is never recomputed for retries, late arrivals or partial payments.

Loan financial transactions take a PostgreSQL row lock before reading installment balances. Installments are read/updated exclusively under their parent's loan lock, and every competing financial writer uses that same lock. Arrival at the financial boundary is the order in which transactions acquire this lock; delivery timestamps do not reorder posted history. This order need not equal network start order across independent receiver replicas. A bank business timestamp more than seven days before receipt is flagged for review without changing the original timestamp.

Before allocation, recognize each newly eligible installment's scheduled interest once: debit InterestReceivable, credit InterestIncome. Each successful payment produces one balanced journal: debit BankClearing for the full amount, credits PrincipalReceivable for allocated principal, InterestReceivable for allocated scheduled interest, and UnappliedCustomerCredit for excess. Journal identities are unique and application code rejects update/delete of posted journals/postings. Allocation totals, current principal and journal debits/credits are checked in integration tests. An already applied success remains posted if a conflicting FAILED outcome arrives.

The demo excludes daily accrual, late fees, refunds, early payoff re-amortization, settlement cash finality and production loan-origination underwriting. Bank clearing is simulated. No real money, SMS or email is involved.
